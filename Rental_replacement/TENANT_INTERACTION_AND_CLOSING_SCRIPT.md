## 🏡 2. Site Visit Protocol Checklist

When prospective tenants come to view the room:

1. **Highlight Sunlight & Ventilation First:** Keep curtains drawn back and balcony doors open to showcase natural light.
2. **Showcase WFH Workstation:** Demonstrate the dual monitor space, ergonomic chair, and high-speed Wi-Fi router.
3. **Show Balcony Access:** Point out the magnetic mosquito mesh door—explain how nice it is during Bangalore evenings.
4. **Kitchen Tour:** Show the modular storage, clean RO purifier, and gas setup. Mention: *"Cook prepares fresh meals here daily!"*
5. **Bathroom Inspection:** Turn on the geyser to show instant hot water and clean tiles.
6. **Closing Question:** *"We have 2 other visits scheduled today. If you like the place, you can secure it with a ₹5,000-₹10,000 token deposit today."*

## 📝 3. Safe Deposit Handover & Landlord Agreement Addendum

To ensure you receive your ₹33,000 deposit back cleanly without landlord disputes:

### Step-by-Step Deposit & Setup Money Collection Process:
1. **Token Amount (₹10,000):** Collect upon deal confirmation from incoming tenant. Give written receipt.
2. **Remaining Amount (₹50,000):** Collect prior to key handover on move-in day (₹23k remaining deposit + ₹27k setup cost).
3. **Landlord Notification:** Inform landlord/owner over WhatsApp/Email:
   > *"Hi [Owner Name], as discussed, I am relocating to another city. [New Tenant Name] will be replacing me in the flat starting [Date]. They will take over my deposit share of ₹33,000."*
