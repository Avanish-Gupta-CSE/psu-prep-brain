## 🏛️ About STPI

Software Technology Parks of India (STPI) is a premier Science & Technology autonomous society under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India. Established in 1991 by merging three pioneering Software Technology Parks at Bengaluru, Bhubaneswar, and Pune (set up in 1989–90), STPI was created with the primary mandate of promoting software exports and fostering the growth of the IT/ITeS industry across India.

STPI acts as a single-window facilitation agency, providing statutory and value-added services to software exporting units. It implements the Software Technology Park (STP) Scheme and the Electronics and Hardware Technology Park (EHTP) Scheme of the Government of India. Over the decades, it has evolved from an export promoter into a full-fledged technology ecosystem builder, nurturing startups, driving R&D, and spearheading India's emergence as a global software product nation.

### 🌟 Vision & Mission
*   **Vision**: To make India a Global Information Technology Power and one of the largest generators and exporters of software in the world.
*   **Mission**: To boost software exports and services from the country by promoting the IT/ITeS industry.

## 🎯 Objectives of STPI

*   Promote the development and export of software and software services including IT/ITeS/Bio-IT.
*   Provide statutory and other promotional services to exporters by implementing STP/EHTP Schemes and other schemes entrusted by Government.
*   Provide data communication services including value-added services to IT/ITeS related industries.
*   Promote MSMEs by creating a conducive environment for entrepreneurship in IT/ITeS.
*   Establish and manage infrastructure resources — international communication, data centres, incubation facilities — for 100% export-oriented units.
*   Undertake export promotional activities: technology assessments, market analysis, workshops, exhibitions, and conferences.
*   Facilitate specialised training in niche areas.
*   Work closely with State Governments; act as interface between Industry and Government.
*   Promote entrepreneurship through incubation programmes, seed funds, IP development, and awareness programmes.
*   Promote quality and security standards in the IT industry.
*   Work jointly with venture capitalists to provide financial assistance to IT industries.

## 🚀 Key Functions & Services Portfolio

### 1. Statutory Services (STP & EHTP Schemes)
*   **STP Scheme**: A 100% export-oriented scheme for computer software development and export. It integrates 100% EOU and EPZ concepts with the Science/Technology Park model. It provides single-point contact and approvals under single-window clearance. STP units can be set up anywhere in India; projects < Rs. 100 million are cleared by the jurisdictional STPI.
*   **EHTP Scheme**: Designed for the export of electronic hardware—manufacture, R&D, testing, and calibration. It allows customs duty-free imports of capital goods.

### 2. Internet / DataCom Services
*   High-bandwidth internet connectivity and value-added data communication services to IT/ITeS units.

### 3. Incubation Services
*   Ready-to-use workspace with workstations, cubicles, manager cabins, and conference halls.
*   No gestation period, no capital investment required; units can start from Day 1.
*   Available for startups, aspiring entrepreneurs, and MNCs.

### 4. Data Centre & Cloud Services (Ananta)
*   **Data Centre**: 5 Tier-3 data centres providing secure, reliable hosting and co-location services.
*   **Ananta**: STPI's own cloud platform offering cloud infrastructure to IT units.

### 5. Security Audit (VAPT)
*   Information Security Audit Services including Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing.

### 6. Other Services
*   **CRO Surveillance Services**: Compliance monitoring and surveillance services for registered units.
*   **PMC Services**: Project Management and Consultancy services at national and international levels.
*   **NGIS**: Next Generation Incubation Scheme (Rs. 95.03 Cr budget; seed funding up to Rs. 25 lakh for 300 startups across 12 Tier-II cities).

## 🔌 Key Government Schemes Implemented by STPI

1.  **STP Scheme**: 100% EOU scheme for software development and export.
2.  **EHTP Scheme**: 100% export scheme for electronic hardware manufacture, R&D, testing, and calibration.
3.  **NGIS**: Rs. 95.03 Cr scheme; seed funding up to Rs. 25 lakh; 12 Tier-II city incubation hubs.
4.  **BPO Promotion Scheme**: Promotes Business Process Outsourcing operations from India, extending STPI's facilitation to the broader ITeS sector.
5.  **EMC 2.0 (Electronics Manufacturing Cluster 2.0)**: MeitY scheme for creating electronics manufacturing clusters; STPI plays a facilitation/implementation role.
6.  **NPSP 2019 (National Policy on Software Products)**: Aligned with STPI's goal to transform India into a software product nation.

## 🏛️ Governance & Structure

*   **Governing Body**: Governing Council chaired by the **Secretary, MeitY**.
*   **CEO / Head**: Director General (currently **Shri Arvind Kumar**).
*   **Jurisdictional Directorates**: 14 Jurisdictional Directorates covering all states.
*   **Fund Management**: STPI Fund: minimum 60% in public sector banks or as prescribed by the Government.
*   **Regulatory Compliance**: Operates under the Societies Registration Act; policies governed by MeitY.
