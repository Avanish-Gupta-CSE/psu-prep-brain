## 🏛️ 1. Organizational Overview

*   **What is STPI?** Software Technology Parks of India (STPI) is an autonomous society established in 1991 under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).
*   **Current Leadership**: Shri Arvind Kumar (Director General).
*   **Footprint**: 73 centres across India, with an impressive 65 centres located in Tier-II and Tier-III cities to decentralize IT growth.
*   **Core Mandate**: To boost IT/ITeS exports, foster tech innovation, and transition India from an IT services hub to a "Software Product Nation" (aligned with the National Policy on Software Products 2019).

---

## 🚀 2. Major Schemes & Initiatives

As a Scientist 'B', you will likely be involved in managing or evaluating these programs:
*   **STP & EHTP Schemes**: Statutory schemes providing single-window clearance, 100% customs duty exemption, and 100% FDI for software and electronics hardware exporters.
*   **Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS)**: A massive initiative across 12 Tier-II cities providing plug-and-play infrastructure, mentoring, and up to ₹25 Lakh seed funding to tech startups.
*   **CHUNAUTI**: A rigorous screening challenge under NGIS to select startups for incubation.
*   **GENESIS**: "Gen-Next Support for Innovative Startups" – an initiative targeting deep-tech startups in Tier-II and III cities.
