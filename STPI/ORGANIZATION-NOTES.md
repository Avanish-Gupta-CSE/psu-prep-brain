## 🏛️ 1. Core Profile & Mandate

*   **Establishment**: June 5, 1991, as an autonomous society under the **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**, Government of India.
*   **Primary Objective**: To promote, encourage, and boost software and electronic hardware exports from India.
*   **Distributed Growth**: STPI has expanded from 3 centres to **71 centres** across India, out of which **63 centres are in Tier-II and Tier-III cities**. This is the key vehicle for dispersing IT/ITeS/ESDM industries away from major metros.
*   **Key Statutory Role**: Administers the **STP** and **EHTP** schemes, issues **Softex Certification** (for tracking software exports), and provides single-window clearance and customs bonding for export-oriented units.

---

## 📈 2. Statutory Schemes (100% Export-Oriented)

### A. Software Technology Park (STP) Scheme
*   **Nature**: A 100% export-oriented scheme for the development and export of computer software, including professional IT services.
*   **Integration**: Merges the concepts of 100% Export Oriented Units (EOU), Export Processing Zones (EPZ), and international Science/Technology Parks.
*   **Key Benefits**:
    *   Duty-free imports of capital goods (hardware/software) required for export operations.
    *   100% foreign equity utility allowed.
    *   Single-point contact services for member units to accelerate export operations.
    *   Units can be set up anywhere in India (unlike SEZs, which are geographically restricted).
    *   *Note*: Direct income tax exemptions (under Sec 10A/10B) have expired, but duty-free imports and Softex digitization remain major cost-savers.

### B. Electronic Hardware Technology Park (EHTP) Scheme
*   **Nature**: A 100% export-oriented scheme for the development, prototyping, and export of electronic hardware.
*   **Permitted Activities**:
    *   Manufacturing electronic hardware items for export.
    *   Integrated manufacturing of both hardware and software.
    *   R&D, testing, calibration, and maintenance services for export-bound electronics.
*   **Key Benefits**:
    *   Duty-free procurement of capital goods and raw materials from domestic or international sources.
    *   Simplified minimum export performance norms (only requires maintaining a **Positive Net Foreign Exchange (NFE)** earnings over a 5-year block).

## 🚀 3. STPINEXT Initiatives (Section 8 SPV)

*   **What is it?**: **AIC STPINEXT Initiatives** (referred to as STPINEXT) is a Section 8 Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) established by STPI.
*   **Role**: It acts as the entrepreneurial and operational arm of STPI, running modern incubation programs, collaborative Centers of Entrepreneurship (CoEs), national startup challenges, and venture/mentor networks.
*   **Key Programs**:
    *   **OctaNE**: Open Challenge Program (OCP) for Northeast India startups.
    *   **BuildForBharat**: IoT Startup Challenge in partnership with Jio Things (JTL).
    *   **IndiaAI Innovation Challenge**: Nurturing AI startups solving national-scale problems.

## 💡 4. Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS)

*   **Vision**: MeitY’s flagship, futuristic incubation scheme implemented by STPI to support software product startups (including embedded electronics) with a synergized pan-India approach.
*   **Budgetary Outlay**: **Rs. 95.03 Crores** over 3 years.
*   **Target**: To support and incentivize **300 select startups** in **12 Tier-II locations** across India.
*   **12 NGIS Locations**:
    1. Agartala
    2. Bhilai
    3. Bhopal
    4. Bhubaneswar
    5. Dehradun
    6. Guwahati
    7. Jaipur
    8. Lucknow
    9. Prayagraj
    10. Mohali
    11. Patna
    12. Vijayawada
*   **Financial Incentives**:
    *   **Pre-Incubation (Ideation Stage)**: Select startups/interns are paid **Rs. 10,000 per month** for up to 6 months to refine their business plans.
    *   **Seed Funding**: Direct seed funding of **up to Rs. 25.00 Lakhs** per beneficiary startup based on innovation, novelty, and business viability.
*   **Key Facilities**:
    *   Access to **Software Product Security Testing (SPST)** facility for full-fledged security and vulnerability testing.
    *   Ready-to-work plug-and-play incubation space.
    *   Cloud credits from leading third-party providers.
