## 🏛️ SECTION 1: Organizational Genesis & Core Mandate

Software Technology Parks of India (STPI) was established in 1991 as an autonomous society under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).

*   **The Big Shift**: Historically, STPI was created to boost IT/ITeS exports through statutory single-window clearances. Today, its mandate has aggressively shifted toward making India a **"Software Product Nation"** (aligning with the **National Policy on Software Products 2019**) by incubating deep-tech startups and fostering Intellectual Property (IP) creation.
*   **Scale and Reach**: STPI operates 73 centres across India. Crucially, 65 of these are in Tier-II and Tier-III cities (like Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Dehradun). STPI's primary goal is to **democratize tech growth** so it isn't limited to Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune.

## 🚀 SECTION 2: The Core Schemes (Must-Know)

As a Scientist 'B', you will be directly or indirectly evaluating, managing, or mentoring initiatives under these schemes. You must know these acronyms:

### 1. NGIS (Next Generation Incubation Scheme)
*   **What it is**: A ₹95.03 Crore futuristic incubation scheme focused on 12 Tier-II locations (Agartala, Bhilai, Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Dehradun, Guwahati, Jaipur, Lucknow, Prayagraj, Mohali, Patna, Vijayawada).
*   **Target**: To support 300 innovative software product startups over 3 years.
*   **Funding**: Provides a seed fund of up to ₹25 Lakh per startup and ₹10,000/month intern stipends during pre-incubation.

### 2. CHUNAUTI (Challenge Hunt Under NGIS for Advanced Uninhibited Technology Intervention)
*   **What it is**: The actual screening/hackathon process used to select startups for the NGIS program.
*   **Note**: STPI regularly launches iterations of this (e.g., CHUNAUTI 8.0/9.0) focusing on emerging tech, EdTech, FinTech, and AgriTech.

### 3. LEAP AHEAD (Launchpad for Entrepreneurs towards Accelerated growth and Pioneering AHEAD)
*   **What it is**: Recently launched its 2nd Edition (late 2024/2025). This is for startups in the scaling or growth stage (not just ideation).
*   **Benefits**: Offers intensive 3-month mentorship, networking with top global investors, and potential funding of up to ₹1 Crore.

### 4. GENESIS (Gen-Next Support for Innovative Startups)
*   **What it is**: A massive initiative with a ₹490 Crore outlay aimed at nurturing over 1,600 deep-tech startups specifically in Tier-II and Tier-III cities over the next five years.

### 5. STP & EHTP Schemes
*   **STP (Software Technology Park)**: Provides 100% customs duty exemption on capital goods and allows 100% FDI for software exporters.
*   **EHTP (Electronics Hardware Technology Park)**: The hardware equivalent, designed to boost the ESDM (Electronic System Design and Manufacturing) sector.
