## The NOC question — answered

The ad's Annexure-IV is titled:

> *"FORM OF CERTIFICATE TO BE SUBMITTED BY **CENTRAL GOVERNMENT CIVILIAN EMPLOYEES SEEKING AGE-RELAXATION**"*

It certifies 3 years' regular service and exists purely as the age-relaxation vehicle for serving central government employees.

**You are not a Central Government civilian employee** (UCO officers are employees of a body corporate), and **you are not claiming age relaxation** (26 against a 33 limit).

→ **Attach no NOC. Fill form item 18 honestly. Raise nothing at UCO.**
