Core finding

The PSU cannot deliver a 2027 exit — the exam→shortlist→interview→joining chain is seven to ten months by itself. 2027 is a corporate deadline. The PSU becomes the upgrade that arrives in 2028–29.

What changed in this analysis. The original plan treated GATE as the spine. The data says otherwise: only two Maharatnas take GATE for Computer Science, with a combined ~50–70 seats per year against 211,020 candidates. The GATE syllabus is still the correct curriculum — it is the union of every PSU technical paper — but the GATE rank is not a plan.
