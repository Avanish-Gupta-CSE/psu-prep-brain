## 📝 Selection Scheme (75% CBT + 25% Interview)

1. **Online Objective Written Examination (75% Weightage):**
   - **Total Questions:** 120 Questions
   - **Total Marks:** 150 Marks
   - **Duration:** 120 Minutes (2 Hours)
   - **Negative Marking:** 1/4th (0.25) marks deduction for wrong answers.
2. **Personal Interview (25% Weightage):**
   - Shortlisted candidates in the ratio of 1:3 or 1:4 called for interview at New Delhi / Corporate Office.
