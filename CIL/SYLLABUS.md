# Coal India Limited (CIL) Management Trainee (Systems) — Syllabus & Pattern (2026)

This document outlines the official syllabus and exam pattern for the **Coal India Limited (CIL) Management Trainee (Systems)** recruitment exam.

---

## 📊 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

| Paper | Section / Content | Questions | Marks | Duration | Negative Marking |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Paper I** | General Aptitude (Quant, Reasoning, English, GK) | 100 | 100 | <br>3 Hours<br>(Single Sitting) | **None** (Attempt all 200!) |
| **Paper II** | Professional Knowledge (Computer Science & IT) | 100 | 100 | | **None** |
| **Total** | | **200** | **200** | | |

*   **Mode**: Computer-Based Test (CBT) only.
*   **Selection Process**: **CBT Only — NO INTERVIEW!** Selection is based purely on CBT merit.
*   **Bilingual**: Questions appear in English & Hindi (except the English section).
*   **Qualifying Cutoff**: Minimum **35% (35/100)** in each paper for OBC-NCL. (Our target is **85+ in each paper**).

## 🧠 Paper I: General Aptitude — 100 Questions

### 1. Numerical Ability / Quantitative Aptitude
- Number Systems, HCF & LCM
- Simplification, Decimals & Fractions
- Percentages, Ratio & Proportion
- Average, Age Problems
- Profit, Loss & Discount
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Time & Work, Time, Speed & Distance
- Mixtures & Alligations
- Mensuration (2D & 3D)
- Data Interpretation (Tables, Line Graphs, Bar Graphs, Pie Charts)

### 2. Reasoning Ability
- Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations
- Direction Sense Test
- Syllogisms
- Seating Arrangements (Linear, Circular, Grid)
- Puzzles (Tabulation, Floor, Box)
- Series (Number, Alphabet, Alphanumeric)
- Analogies & Classifications
- Clocks & Calendars
- Non-Verbal Reasoning (Pattern completion, Mirror/Water Images, Paper folding)

### 3. General English
- Spotting Errors & Sentence Correction
- Fill in the Blanks (Prepositions, Articles, Tenses, Verbs)
- Synonyms, Antonyms, and Homonyms
- Idioms & Phrases
- One-Word Substitutions
- Spelling Test (Detecting Misspelt Words)
- Sentence Rearrangement / Parajumbles
- Reading Comprehension Passages

### 4. General Awareness & Current Affairs
- National & International Current Affairs (Last 6-12 months)
- Indian Constitution, Polity & Governance
- Geography (India & World)
- History of India & Indian National Movement
- Indian Economy & Economic Scene
- General Science & Technology
- Books & Authors, Awards & Honors, Sports & Games
- Basic Coal & Energy Sector General Knowledge (CIL facts, Maharatna status, coal production basics)

## 💻 Paper II: Professional Knowledge (Computer Science & IT) — 100 Questions

Since CIL does not release a granular topic-wise syllabus, Paper II is mapped directly to the **GATE Computer Science & IT syllabus** as a comprehensive reference, with additional focus on software engineering and web technologies.

### 1. Digital Logic
- Boolean algebra, minimization of Boolean functions.
- Combinational circuits (multiplexers, decoders, encoders, adders).
- Sequential circuits (latches, flip-flops, registers, counters).
- Number representations and computer arithmetic (fixed and floating point).

### 2. Computer Organization & Architecture (COA)
- Machine instructions and addressing modes.
- ALU, data-path, and control unit.
- Instruction pipelining, pipeline hazards.
- Memory hierarchy: cache, main memory, virtual memory.
- Secondary storage, I/O interface (interrupt and DMA mode).

### 3. Programming & Data Structures
- Programming in C.
- Recursion.
- Arrays, stacks, queues, linked lists, trees, binary search trees, binary heaps, graphs.

### 4. Algorithms
- Searching, sorting, hashing.
- Asymptotic worst-case time and space complexity.
- Algorithm design techniques: greedy, dynamic programming, divide-and-conquer.
- Graph traversals (BFS, DFS), minimum spanning trees, shortest paths.

### 5. Theory of Computation (TOC) & Compiler Design
- Regular expressions and finite automata.
- Context-free grammars and pushdown automata.
- Regular and context-free languages, pumping lemma.
- Turing machines and undecidability.
- Lexical analysis, parsing, syntax-directed translation.
- Runtime environments, intermediate code generation, local optimization.

### 6. Operating Systems (OS)
- System calls, processes, threads, CPU scheduling.
- Inter-process communication, concurrency and synchronization, semaphores, critical section, deadlocks.
- Memory management: paging, segmentation, virtual memory, page replacement algorithms.
- File systems, directory structure, disk scheduling.

### 7. Database Management Systems (DBMS)
- ER-model, Relational model (relational algebra, tuple calculus).
- Database design: integrity constraints, normal forms (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF).
- Query languages: SQL.
- Transactions and concurrency control: ACID properties, serializability, locking protocols.

### 8. Computer Networks (CN)
- Concept of layering: OSI and TCP/IP Protocol Suites.
- Basics of packet, circuit and virtual circuit-switching.
- Data link layer: framing, error detection, Medium Access Control, Ethernet bridging.
- Network layer: routing protocols, IPv4/IPv6, CIDR, subnetting, fragmentation.
- Transport layer: flow control, congestion control, TCP/UDP, sockets.
- Application layer protocols: DNS, SMTP, HTTP, HTTPS, FTP.

### 9. Software Engineering & Web Technologies
- Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) models (Waterfall, Spiral, Agile/Scrum).
- Software requirements, UML diagrams, testing (unit, integration, system, black-box, white-box).
- Web Technologies: HTML, CSS, XML, JavaScript basics, client-server model.
