# IFFCO Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET) — Computer Science Syllabus & Pattern (2026)

This document outlines the official syllabus and exam pattern for the **Indian Farmers Fertiliser Cooperative Limited (IFFCO) Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET) — Computer Science** preliminary online assessment.

---

## 📊 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

| Section | Content | Questions | Marks | Duration | Negative Marking |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Section A** | Technical (Computer Science & IT) | 80 | 80 | <br>60 Minutes<br>(1 Hour) | **None** (Attempt all 100!) |
| **Section B** | General Aptitude & Non-Technical | 40 | 40 | | **None** |
| **Total** | | **120** | **120** | | |

*   **Preliminary Exam Mode**: Online Computer-Based Test (CBT) in an **open environment** (taken on your own laptop with internet and webcam enabled).
*   **Backtracking**: **Allowed** (Candidates can navigate back and forth, flag questions, and clear answers).
*   **Final Exam Mode**: Shortlisted candidates from the preliminary exam will appear for a controlled-environment CBT at designated centers.
*   **Selection Process**: Preliminary CBT ➔ Final CBT ➔ Personal Interview (PI) ➔ Medical Examination.

## 💻 Section A: Technical (Computer Science & IT) — 80 Questions

### 1. Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)
- **Data Structures**: Arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees (binary trees, BST, AVL, heaps), graphs (BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, MST), hashing.
- **Algorithms**: Sorting and searching algorithms, asymptotic analysis, greedy algorithms, dynamic programming, divide and conquer.

### 2. Database Management Systems (DBMS)
- **Relational Databases**: Relational model, database design (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF), functional dependencies.
- **Queries & Transactions**: SQL queries, transactions and concurrency control (ACID properties, serializability, locking).

### 3. Operating Systems (OS)
- **Process Management**: Processes, threads, CPU scheduling, inter-process communication, synchronization, semaphores, deadlocks.
- **Memory & Storage**: Memory management, virtual memory, paging, segmentation, file systems.

### 4. Computer Networks (CN)
- **Network Models**: OSI and TCP/IP protocol suites.
- **Protocols & Routing**: IP addressing (IPv4/IPv6, CIDR, subnetting), routing algorithms, TCP/UDP, DNS, SMTP, HTTP, HTTPS, FTP.
- **Security**: Basic network security, firewalls, cryptography basics.

### 5. Basics of Machine Learning (ML)
- **Supervised Learning**: Linear regression, logistic regression, decision trees, support vector machines (SVM).
- **Unsupervised Learning**: Clustering (K-Means), dimensionality reduction (PCA).
- **Neural Networks**: Basic concepts, activation functions, training process.

## 🧠 Section B: General Aptitude & Non-Technical — 40 Questions

### 1. Numerical Ability
- Percentages & Ratios
- Profit, Loss & Discount
- Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns
- Time, Speed & Distance
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Data Interpretation (Tables, Bar Charts, Pie Charts)

### 2. Reasoning Ability
- Coding-Decoding
- Syllogisms
- Seating Arrangements (Linear, Circular)
- Number Series, Alphabet Series
- Analogies & Directions

### 3. General Awareness (High Focus on Agriculture & Cooperatives)
- **Agriculture**: Indian agriculture, crop seasons (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid), major crops, soil types, irrigation, fertilizers (Urea, NPK, DAP), green revolution.
- **Cooperatives**: History of cooperative movement in India, cooperative principles, major cooperatives (IFFCO, KRIBHCO, AMUL), cooperative societies act.
- **General**: Indian Economy, Current Affairs, Geography, and General Politics.

### 4. English & Communication Skills
- English Grammar (Tenses, Prepositions, Conjunctions)
- Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word substitutions)
- Sentence Correction & Fill in the Blanks
- Reading Comprehension basics
