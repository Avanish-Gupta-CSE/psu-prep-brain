## 3. Prescribed Eligibility Criteria

1. **Educational Qualification:**
   - B.E. / B.Tech or equivalent degree in **Computer Science & Engineering** / **Information Technology** with an aggregate minimum of **65% marks** or **CGPA 6.84 / 10**.
   - The qualifying degree must have been completed within the stipulated course duration.
2. **Age Limit (as on 16 September 2026):**
   - General (UR) / EWS: Maximum 28 Years (Born on or after 17.09.1998)
   - OBC (NCL): Maximum 31 Years (3 years relaxation)
   - SC / ST: Maximum 33 Years (5 years relaxation)
   - Ex-Servicemen & PwBD: As per Govt. of India rules.

## 4. Selection Procedure & Pattern

1. **Written Test (CBT):**
   - Conducted across **37 cities in India**.
   - **Part A (Discipline Specific):**
     - 80 Multiple Choice Questions (Core CS topics aligned with GATE standard).
     - Time: 90 Minutes.
     - Marking: +1 mark for correct answer, -0.33 (1/3) mark for incorrect answer.
   - **Part B (Aptitude / Ability):**
     - 15 Multiple Choice Questions (20 Marks total).
     - Tests numerical reasoning, logical ability, diagrammatic reasoning, abstract reasoning, and deduction.
     - Time: 30 Minutes.
     - Marking: No negative marking.
2. **Interview Shortlisting:**
   - Candidates are shortlisted based on Part A + Part B written test performance in the ratio of **1 : 5** per vacancy category.
3. **Final Selection Merit:**
   - Written Test Score (50% Weightage) + Personal Interview Score (50% Weightage).
   - Minimum qualifying mark in Interview: 50% for UR/EWS, 40% for OBC/SC/ST/PwBD.
