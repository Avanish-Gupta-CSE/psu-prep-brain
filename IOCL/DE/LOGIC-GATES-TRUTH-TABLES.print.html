QUICK-FIRE BOXES — print page 2

De Morgan · universal recipes · XOR/XNOR magic · traps

De Morgan — NAND and NOR duals

(A·B)′ = A′ + B′

(A+B)′ = A′ · B′

NAND = OR of inverted inputs · NOR = AND of inverted inputs

Universal recipes

NOT = A↑A = A↓A

OR from NAND: (A↑A) ↑ (B↑B)

AND from NAND: (A↑B) ↑ (A↑B)

AND from NOR: (A↓A) ↓ (B↓B)

OR from NOR: (A↓B) ↓ (A↓B)

XOR magic (parity gate)

X⊕0 = X · X⊕1 = X′ (programmable inverter)

X⊕X = 0 · X⊕X′ = 1 · A⊕B⊕B = A

n-chain XOR = 1 iff odd number of 1s

XNOR (equality detector)

1 iff inputs equal · complement of XOR

n-chain XNOR = 1 iff even number of 1s

Associative (unlike NAND/NOR!)

★ Traps that appear in MCQs

  • X⊕X = 0 — XOR is NOT idempotent (same for XNOR: X⊙X = 1)
  • NAND/NOR commute, but are NOT associative and NOT distributive
  • XNOR IS associative — (A⊙B)⊙C = A⊙B⊙C
  • Distributive both ways: only AND and OR
  • Universal: NAND alone, NOR alone — XOR is NOT universal

40-second recall script — "Six basic gates: AND (1 iff all 1s), OR (1 iff any 1), NAND/NOR their inverses — both universal. XOR = parity, 1 when inputs differ; XNOR = equality. Properties: all commutative; associative = AND, OR, XOR, XNOR — NAND/NOR are not; idempotent = only AND, OR since X XOR X = 0. De Morgan: (AB)′ = A′+B′. Example: full adder Sum = A⊕B⊕Cin, Carry = AB + Cin(A⊕B)."
