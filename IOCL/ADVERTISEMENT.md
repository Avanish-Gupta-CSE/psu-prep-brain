## 3. Selection Methodology

1. **Computer Based Test (CBT):**
   - **Total Questions:** 100 Objective Questions (MCQs)
   - **Duration:** 120 Minutes (2 Hours)
   - **Structure:**
     - **Section A:** Professional / Domain Knowledge (Computer Science & IT)
     - **Section B:** General Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Numerical Ability & English
   - **Sectional Qualifying Marks:**
     - General / OBC-NCL / EWS: 40% in Section A, 40% in Section B, 45% aggregate
     - SC / ST: 35% in each section, 40% aggregate
2. **Shortlisting for Next Rounds:**
   - Candidates qualifying the CBT sectional and overall thresholds are ranked in descending order of CBT scores.
   - Shortlisted candidates are invited for Group Discussion (GD), Group Task (GT), and Personal Interview (PI).
3. **Service Agreement Bond:**
   - Selected candidates execute a Service Agreement to serve the Corporation for a minimum of 3 years.
