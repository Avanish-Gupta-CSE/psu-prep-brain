## 6. Verdict

**Accepting UCO Bank was the right call.** The reasoning holds on four independent grounds:

1. **It was the only real offer.** One selection from ~25 tracks. Declining it to chase a pending exam would have been rejecting a certainty for a ~4% historical hit rate.
2. **The pay was never the sacrifice it looked like.** Effective value of ~₹13.4-14.4 LPA against a ₹11.0 LPA CTC, with rent fully absorbed by the bank.
3. **The exit was executed without risk.** Written extension secured before resignation. That is the single most important thing done correctly in this entire cycle.
4. **It is a floor, not a ceiling.** The bond expires at age 28. ISRO (up to 35), CERT-In, SEBI (33), BSNL (33), CONCOR, IOCL and the rest remain open well past that. Nothing has been foreclosed.

### The Correct Framing

> UCO Bank is not the destination -- it is the **guaranteed base camp**. It converts an unstable "will I ever clear one?" position into "I am a confirmed officer-cadre software developer, and every further attempt is now upside with zero downside."

The prior position was a private-sector engineer with no PSU offer, writing exams under financial and time pressure. The new position is a confirmed Gazetted-equivalent officer with 10-6 hours, free housing, and the freedom to keep writing HAL, IOCL, ISRO and CONCOR from a position of complete security. **That is a strictly better place to attempt from.**
