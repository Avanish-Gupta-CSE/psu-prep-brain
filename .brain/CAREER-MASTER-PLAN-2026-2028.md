| **PL** | Locked till ~Sept 2028 — irrelevant to Feb 2028 |

### 12c-4. The untapped instrument: compensatory off
Banking-IT departments work month-end / quarter-end / DR-drill weekends. Where a holiday or off-Saturday is worked, **comp off is usually credited**. At ~1/month that is **~12 extra days/year — a second CL allowance.**
- **Ask HR:** comp-off policy for holidays/weekends worked in DIT.
- **Rule:** bank comp-offs, do not spend them casually. Every comp off is worth 2× a CL because it attaches to weekends freely.

### 12c-4b. Half-day CL — the quiet multiplier (user-confirmed 21 Oct)
**12 CL = 24 half-days.** More useful than the count is *what halves do to the optics*.

| Use | Full day or half? | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ISRO Thu 29 Oct (Shift 1)** | **Half-day (first half)** — *if* the centre is near | Exam ends ~noon; back at desk by ~14:00. **Saves 0.5 CL and signals minimum disruption** |
| **Document verification (Kolkata)** | **Half-day** | ~3 h of work, not a day |
| **Interview in Kolkata** | **Half-day → full if it overruns** | Most local interviews are 3–4 h |
| **Interview out of town** | **Full day** | Travel + buffer |
| **Study blocks (Jan 2028, Durga Puja)** | **Full days only** | Deep work does not halve well — 2 halves ≈ 1.3 full days of output, not 2 |

**The yield table (days off per 1 CL):**
| Construction | Days off | Yield |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Full day + holiday + weekend (Jan block) | 4 | **4.0** |
| **2 half-days bracketing a weekend** (Fri 2nd-half + Mon 1st-half) | ~3.5 | **3.5** |
| 1 full day adjacent to a weekend | 3 | 3.0 |
| 1 half-day before an off-Saturday | 2.5 | 5.0 per CL, but only 0.5 CL spent |

**Strategic value — this is the real point:** a half-day is *invisible*. "He took Tuesday afternoon" never reaches HR; "he took nine consecutive days" does. **Use halves to build the sanction track record that makes the 2028 block approvable.**

**And split the 2028 ask into two requests** (22–30 Jan, then 31 Jan–6 Feb) rather than one nine-day application. Two routine asks beat one alarming one.

**Ask HR:** monthly cap on half-days? can two halves be combined into a full day? can a half-day be prefixed/suffixed to a holiday?

### 12c-5. The real gate is sanction, not the number
4 CL "with approval first" means **approvability is the binding constraint**, not entitlement. Protocol:
1. **Ask 2–3 weeks ahead, in writing**, with the admit card attached. Never the day before.
2. **One day at a time** until a track record exists — blocks look like job-hunting, single days look like logistics.
3. **Avoid month-end / quarter-end / DR-drill weeks** — that is when DIT says no.
4. **Ask the reporting officer first, then HR.** Never route around the RO.
5. **The first approval sets the precedent.** Burn it on ISRO (one day, documented, obviously legitimate) so the Jan-2027 block reads as routine.
6. **Log every request and outcome.** A pattern of "approved, returned on time, no disruption" is what makes Jan 2028's 9-day block approvable.

### 12d. The 3-day rule
**Never spend 1 CL to buy 1 day.** A CL should always convert into a 3–5 day block:

| Block | Construction | Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sat 23 – Tue 26 Jan 2027** | Sat off + Sun + **Mon 1 CL** + **Republic Day (Tue)** | **4 days for 1 CL** — GATE 2027 final push |
| **Durga Puja 2027 (~early Oct)** | 5–6 consecutive holidays + 2 CL | **7–8 day block** — biggest study block of the year; reserve for GATE 2028 syllabus |
| **Sat 22 – Wed 26 Jan 2028** | Sat/Sun + 2 CL + **Republic Day (Wed)** | 5-day final sprint, ~2 weeks before GATE 2028 |

⚠️ Wasted in 2027: **15 Aug (Sun)**, **25 Dec (Sat)**, **2 Oct (Sat)** — holidays that buy nothing.

### 12e. The 3-question exam filter (kills the "apply to everything" spiral)
1. **Is it on a Sunday / holiday?** → if yes, costs nothing.
2. **Does it test the technical paper (CS/IT), not aptitude-only?** → if no, it hits the known weakness for zero return.
3. **Would you actually accept the job?** → if no, it is a practice test wearing a job's clothes.

**Fails 2 of 3 → do not apply.** Applying is free; *writing* costs a weekend, and weekends are the actual currency.

### 12f. Capacity math (no extra leave)
1.5 h × 5 weekdays (**7.5**) + 4 h Sunday + 2 h off-Saturday (**~13.5 h/wk**) → **~700 h/yr**
\+ holiday blocks (Durga Puja 40 h, Jan block 25 h, misc 60 h) → **~825 h**
\+ 12 CL × 6 h (**72 h**) → **~900 h/yr**

**Leaves are ~8% of study capacity.** The plan needs ~1,000 h over 16 months. It is achievable **without a single extra leave day** — which means the argument "I don't have leaves" is not the real obstacle.
