# Character Certificate — Gazetted Officer / Magistrate / Executive Magistrate / Principal

> **Purpose:** Second character certificate for UCO Bank JMGS-I joining (the first is from KIIT University).
> **Requirement per `Berkadia-Exit-Documents/UCO-BANK-JOINING-DOSSIER.md` §4:** Two separate certificates — one from KIIT + one from a Gazetted Officer / Magistrate / Executive Magistrate / Principal (not related to candidate).
> **Submission:** Physical original at reporting (UCO Bank STC, Salt Lake, Kolkata). No fixed UCO Bank web annexure exists — free-form on official letterhead is accepted (standard for all PSU banks). Get **2 originals** with same current date (Sept 2026, within 6 months of joining).

## Who Can Issue (any one, not related to you)

| Issuer | Example | Seal needed |
|---|---|---|
| Gazetted Officer (Group A/B) | Tehsildar, SDM, BDO, Govt College Principal, PSU Officer, Bank Manager (Scale II+) | Office seal + designation stamp |
| Executive Magistrate | SDM / ADM / Tehsildar (Magistrate powers) | Court/Office seal |
| Principal | Govt / Govt-aided college or school Principal | Institution seal |

> In Bareilly/Kushinagar, the fastest is **Tehsildar, Kasya** or **SDM, Kasya** (same office that issued your OBC-NCL on 10/06/2026) — they already have your records.

---

## Checklist Before You Leave the Office

- [ ] Printed on **official letterhead** (not plain paper)
- [ ] **Current date** — September 2026 (within 6 months of joining)
- [ ] Full name, father's name, DOB, address, KIIT + Roll No. mentioned
- [ ] Conduct line: "good moral character, no criminal/disciplinary case, not related"
- [ ] Purpose line: "for appointment in UCO Bank"
- [ ] **Signature + name + designation + office seal** (all three)
- [ ] **2 originals** signed (bank keeps one, returns one after verification)
- [ ] Carry Aadhaar + old KIIT cert + UCO Bank call letter when you visit

---

## What NOT to Do

- Do not get it from a relative, private company manager, or non-gazetted officer.
- Do not use plain paper — must be letterhead.
- Do not use the old 29.08.2024 date — get fresh Sept 2026 date.
