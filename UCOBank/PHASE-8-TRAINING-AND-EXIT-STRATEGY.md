## 3. 🚪 The NOC Question — the single most misunderstood constraint (read this twice)

**This is the part that should reduce your anxiety the most.**

| Stage of a PSU recruitment | Do you need NOC? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Applying online** | ❌ No (most notifications don't ask) |
| **Appearing in the CBT / written exam** | ❌ **No.** Nobody is informed. You can keep writing every CS/IT PSU exam you like. |
| **Interview / Document Verification / GD** | ✅ **Yes** — this is where NOC (and often a "No Objection" on letterhead) is demanded |
| **Joining** | ✅ Yes — release/technical resignation |

**What this means practically:**
1. **Your exam pipeline is completely open right now.** Keep applying, keep writing. No permission needed.
2. The thing you must actually solve is **leave on exam days** and, later, **NOC when you reach interview stage**.
3. **A rejected NOC is not the end** — it usually means you choose between staying and exiting (and paying the bond). Compute that when you have a *real* offer, not before.

### Leave instruments you should get in writing (next week)
- [ ] The **written leave policy** for probationers (CL / SL / EL accrual + rules)
- [ ] Whether **exam leave / duty leave** exists for departmental or external exams
- [ ] Whether **LWP (leave without pay)** is permitted during probation
- [ ] The **written bond text you signed** — specifically: is the ₹2L waived/relaxed when moving to **another government/PSU** organisation? (Many bonds are.)

> ⚠️ **Until you read the bond deed with your own eyes, treat "₹2L penalty" as an assumption, not a fact.** Ask HR for a copy in writing.

| **Saturday (1st/3rd)** | 4–5 h | Full-length paper or one full subject |
| **Sunday** | 4–5 h | Mock + analysis + weekly review |

**Floor:** 12–15 h/week. **Stretch:** 20 h/week. *This is a 12-month game — consistency beats intensity, because burnout is the real risk now.*

### The five containment behaviours
1. **Give the job two fair months before you judge it.** Reassess on **1 December 2026**, not this week.
2. **Switch your mental frame from "trapped" to "funded".** The salary is financing your exit. Every month = ₹86k + NPS toward the plan.
3. **Engineer the language friction down, not the ambition.** English for all written work. Learn ~20 words of Bengali/Hindi as social lubricant — it costs nothing and removes daily friction.
4. **Build the moat.** Finacle + CBS + enterprise Java at DIT makes you the rare "banking-domain IT" profile. That's leverage inside *and* outside the bank.
5. **Protect the body and sleep.** Sleep ≥ 6.5 h, gym 3×/week (even 40 min). A tired person reads every normal friction as a crisis.

### The one thing you must NOT do
> **Do not resign without an offer in hand.** Not this week, not this month, not this quarter. The audit's rule stands: *exit only when the appointment letter is physically in hand.*
