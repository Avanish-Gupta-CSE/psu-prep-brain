## 3. Waitlist Operation Rules (Confirmed vs Legal Precedents)

* **Future / New Vacancies**: **NO.** Under Supreme Court precedent (*Union of India v. Subit Kumar Das*, *State of Punjab v. Raghbir Chand Sharma*), a reserve list cannot be used as an employment reservoir to absorb future vacancies. New vacancies require fresh advertisements.
* **Current Cycle Dropouts**: **YES.** If a selected candidate in the OBC-NCL / UR merit list for Systems declines the offer, fails Document Verification/Medical, or fails to report on joining day, MSTC HR automatically issues an offer of appointment to the next rank on the reserve list.
* **Operational Action Required**: **Zero active monitoring.** MSTC contacts waitlisted candidates directly via speed post and registered email if the reserve panel operates.
